The game

Up the Leagues is a football club owner game for iPhone: the up-the-leagues save, played from the chairman’s seat. You start in Division 4 as its weakest club, with a field, a few dozen fans and £1,000. Your manager picks the team. Everything else is yours: who to sign and who to sell, how long their contracts run, when to build the stadium and the academy, what to charge at the gate, and how far into the overdraft to go.

Four divisions of 18 clubs. The top two go up, the play-offs send a third, and the bottom three go down. Every player is a card with a drawn face, a rating, an age and a rarity. Win Division 1 to win the game.

What you do

  • Sign and sell. Your scout finds players when the window opens, your manager says what each deal does for the team, and the bank balance has the last word.
  • Intake day. Once a season the academy’s class arrives, face down. Whoever you don’t sign leaves that day.
  • Build the ground. From a field with posts to a stadium, one level at a time.
  • Watch it on the faces. Each match plays out on the players’ cards, or skips to full time.
